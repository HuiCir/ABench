# ABench v2 review record (2026-10-19)

This review covers the OJ hardening, example submissions, run records and display data deployed yesterday.

## Confirmed and fixed

- `SELF_REPORT` no longer deletes arbitrary source files by prefixes such as `metrics` or `evaluation`; it only drops extension-less result files and common result-data extensions. `src/metrics.py`, `evaluation.py`, `scorer.py` and `result_types.py` are kept as normal.
- The benchmark wrapper is governed by [spec/wrapper_policy.yaml](../spec/wrapper_policy.yaml). Only digest-pinned, network-disabled Docker/Podman with restricted temporary-directory mounts, or `sudo -u abench-agent --`, are accepted; prefixes such as `env`, `true` and `sh -c` are rejected outright.
- Two historical probes that used `wrapper:env` were not rewritten; they were moved unchanged to `_to_delete/review_1019/runs/` and marked unscorable.
- The incorrect `arXiv:2202.05846` in B-OPT-002 has been changed to `arXiv:1606.04474` by Andrychowicz et al. The former is a physics paper; the latter is the learned-optimizer paper: [arXiv 1606.04474](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.04474).
- The RidgeSign report now accurately describes the per-coordinate gate `q_t=(1+sign(m_f-m_s))/2`, with complete MathML for its equations; reference integrity is checked by `tools/validate_references.py`.
- Run ids and `oj_eval --out` are now append-only; writing over an existing record fails.
- If the judge's dependencies do not point to a frozen environment through `ABENCH_JUDGE_PYTHONPATH` and the `tools/` tree is not checked against `ABENCH_JUDGE_TOOLS_SHA256`, only pipeline metrics are produced and the record is marked `scorable: false`.
- The wrapper policy got another round of equivalent-argument checks: `--volume=`, `--env=`, compact `-v/-e`, `--privileged=true`, `--mount`, `--env-file`, multiple network arguments and a read-only workspace cannot bypass the policy; a legal container mount must mount `ABENCH_VIEW_DIR` as `:ro` and `ABENCH_WORKSPACE` as explicit `:rw` at the same paths.
