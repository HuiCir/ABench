Tracks and case index

Budget cases all run on one 24GB reference card under the reduced-scale verification protocol, never at the paper's full or large scale; a separate Pro Set provides 4 × H100 and 24 hours for compute-heavy industrial tasks. Every case starts from an anchor paper: the agent retrieves research from before the cutoff, synthesizes it and designs a new method, scored on SOTA margin, method divergence and reference quality. Each track has at least one standard case whose task statement, interface, sandbox guide and judge are complete. The index has five groups: general method tracks, non-mainstream frontier exploration with innovation potential, professional domains such as finance, law, physics, biology and medicine, the industrial Pro Set, and a classic-ML entry level serving as difficulty ladder and calibration.
