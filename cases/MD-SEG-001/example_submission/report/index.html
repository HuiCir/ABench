A Scaled-Down Residual-Encoder U-Net

ABench reference submissionStandard reference for task statement standard v2.1
Abstract

This submission keeps nnU-Net's planning of downsampling and kernel sizes from the patch size and spacing (Isensee et al., 2018) and replaces the plain encoder by residual blocks, after the residual-encoder presets that nnU-Net Revisited found to be the most reliable improvement over the plain U-Net (Isensee et al., 2024) (He et al., 2016). The layout is scaled down (1, 2, 3, 4, 4, 4 blocks per stage, features capped at 256) to stay below 64 million parameters on every task, and the decoder has a single convolution per stage.

1Introduction

Under a fixed recipe, many proposed 3D architectures fail to beat a well-configured U-Net; deeper residual encoders are among the few changes that held up in a controlled comparison (Isensee et al., 2024).

Changes relative to the anchor. Each item states what this submission does differently and where it is in the code.

  1. Residual encoder blocks (1, 2, 3, 4, 4, 4 per stage) replace the two plain convolutions per stage. — network.py: Res, ResEncUNet
  2. Features are capped at 256 instead of 320 and the decoder uses one convolution per stage. — network.py: ResEncUNet

2Related Work

nnU-Net configures a plain U-Net from dataset properties (Isensee et al., 2018); nnU-Net Revisited compares CNN and transformer networks under one recipe and introduces residual-encoder presets (Isensee et al., 2024); residual connections ease the training of deep convolutional networks (He et al., 2016).

3Method

Each residual block has two convolutions with instance normalisation and leaky ReLU and an identity or average-pool plus 1 x 1 projection shortcut. The first block of a stage carries its stride. The decoder upsamples by transposed convolution, concatenates the skip and applies one convolution block; every decoder stage has a 1 x 1 segmentation head for deep supervision.

4Design Rationale

Extra depth goes to the encoder, where the receptive field and the representation are built; a lighter decoder pays for it in memory. Capping the features at 256 keeps the prostate and heart networks at 41 and 54 million parameters.

5Scope and Limitations

Only the encoder changes; normalisation, kernel sizes and the handling of anisotropy are nnU-Net's. Nothing here has been trained in this build.
