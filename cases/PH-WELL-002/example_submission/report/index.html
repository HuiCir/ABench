A Shared Fourier Neural Operator with Parameter FiLM

ABench reference submissionStandard reference for task statement standard v2.1
Abstract

This submission trains one Fourier neural operator (Li et al., 2021) for the three systems of the case, with per-system 1 x 1 lifting and projection layers and FiLM conditioning (Perez et al., 2018) of every layer on the system's normalised physical parameters. It predicts the change of the normalised frame from the last one and is trained on one-step loss, then on two-step unrolled loss with the first step detached, after the pushforward trick (Brandstetter et al., 2022). The systems are from The Well (Ohana et al., 2024).

1Introduction

A surrogate that serves several systems must share what is common (local transport, diffusion, spectral structure) while keeping fields and parameters apart; conditioning on parameters is what lets it interpolate to values it has not seen.

Changes relative to the anchor. Each item states what this submission does differently and where it is in the code.

  1. One FNO is shared by three systems through per-system lifting and projection layers, instead of one model per dataset as in The Well's baselines. — src/fno.py: FNO
  2. FiLM conditioning on the physical parameters, which The Well's baselines do not receive. — src/fno.py: FNO.film

2Related Work

The Well provides the systems and FNO and U-Net baselines trained per dataset (Ohana et al., 2024); FNO learns mixing in a truncated Fourier basis (Li et al., 2021); FiLM conditions features with learned scales and shifts (Perez et al., 2018); the pushforward trick trains on the model's own predictions without backpropagating through them (Brandstetter et al., 2022).

3Method

Inputs are the 4 normalised frames stacked as channels plus two coordinate channels; six blocks of spectral convolution (16 x 16 modes) plus a 1 x 1 convolution, group normalisation and FiLM, with residual connections; width 96. Systems are sampled in turn; AdamW at 1e-3 with cosine decay, 40,000 steps of batch 16, bfloat16.

4Design Rationale

System-specific layers are small (well under 10% of the parameters), so most capacity is shared; predicting the increment keeps the identity easy, which matters for stable rollouts.

5Scope and Limitations

The anisotropic resampling of the radiative layer is ignored, systems are weighted equally regardless of their difficulty, and there is no noise injection. Nothing here has been trained in this build.
