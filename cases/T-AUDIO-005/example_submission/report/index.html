A Weighted Layer Sum and a Linear CTC Layer on a Frozen Speech Encoder

ABench reference submissionStandard reference for task statement standard v2.1
Abstract

This submission is the probing baseline of the SUPERB benchmark (al., 2021) applied to the anchor's pre-trained encoder (Baevski et al., 2020): a softmax-weighted sum of the 13 hidden states of frozen wav2vec 2.0 base, layer-normalised, feeds one linear layer over 28 characters and a blank, trained with CTC (Graves et al., 2006) on the 10 hours of transcribed speech and decoded greedily. It trains 22,314 numbers and is meant as a floor. It has not been run yet: it needs PyTorch and the encoder weights, which were not available where the case was built.

1Introduction

Self-supervised speech encoders learn representations from untranscribed audio; how much a small trained head can extract from a frozen encoder with little transcribed speech, and how well it holds up for harder and noisy speakers, measures what the representations contain.

Changes relative to the anchor. Each item states what this submission does differently and where it is in the code.

  1. A learned softmax weighting over all hidden states instead of the last layer, followed by layer normalisation. — model.py: Head.forward

2Related Work

wav2vec 2.0 pre-trains a Transformer over quantised latent speech units (Baevski et al., 2020); SUPERB evaluates frozen encoders with light heads on weighted layer sums (al., 2021); HuBERT predicts clustered targets (Hsu et al., 2021); CTC trains alignment-free character outputs (Graves et al., 2006); adapters insert small trainable blocks between frozen layers (al., 2019).

3Method

For an utterance, the encoder (frozen, eval mode) returns 13 hidden-state sequences; the head mixes them with softmax(w), applies layer normalisation and a linear map to 29 outputs; CTC loss with AdamW (learning rate 2e-3, 20 epochs, batches of 16 length-sorted utterances); greedy decoding collapses repeats and drops blanks.

4Design Rationale

Phonetic information peaks in middle layers of wav2vec 2.0, so a learned layer mix is better than the last layer; a linear head keeps the trained parameters small and makes the encoder's representation the bottleneck.

5Scope and Limitations

No context beyond the encoder's, no noise augmentation and no decoding beyond greedy; it has not been run yet.
