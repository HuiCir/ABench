An EAGLE-3 Drafter Trained on the Target's Own Answers

ABench reference submissionStandard reference for task statement standard v2.1
Abstract

This submission trains an EAGLE-3 drafter (Li et al., 2025) for Qwen3-8B (Yang & Team), 2025) and serves it with vLLM's exact speculative verification (Leviathan et al., 2023). The drafter fuses the target's hidden states from a low, a middle and a high layer, runs one decoder layer and predicts over the 32,000 target tokens most frequent in its training data; training-time test unrolls it for seven steps on its own features. Training data are the target's greedy answers to UltraChat prompts (Ding et al., 2023).

1Introduction

At batch size 1, decoding is limited by reading the weights once per token; a cheap drafter that proposes several tokens which the target verifies in one pass converts spare compute into fewer target passes, without changing the output.

Changes relative to the anchor. Each item states what this submission does differently and where it is in the code.

  1. The drafter is trained for Qwen3-8B on its own non-thinking answers with a reduced draft vocabulary chosen from those answers. — train.py: main
  2. A chain of 5 speculative tokens served by vLLM instead of EAGLE-3's dynamic draft tree. — serve.py: main

2Related Work

Speculative decoding verifies drafts exactly (Leviathan et al., 2023); EAGLE drafts at the feature level, and EAGLE-3 replaces feature prediction by direct token prediction from fused multi-layer features with training-time test (Li et al., 2025).

3Method

60,000 UltraChat first turns are answered greedily by the target (non-thinking template, at most 1,024 tokens); target features are computed on the fly during training. Loss: sum over the seven unrolled steps of 0.8^k times the cross-entropy against the target's next token; AdamW 5e-5, four epochs, a checkpoint every 2,000 steps. The server proposes 5 tokens per step.

4Design Rationale

Training on the target's own outputs matches the distribution the drafter sees at inference; training-time test trains the drafter on its own earlier predictions, which raises the acceptance of later draft positions.

5Scope and Limitations

A fixed chain of 5 tokens instead of a dynamic tree; the weight names follow vLLM's EAGLE-3 loader at the frozen version and must be checked against it. Nothing here has been run in this build.
